# Image prompts

The banner, the three wing icons and the illustration in the README were generated with OpenAI
image models on 2026-11-07, then checked by eye for stray text, logos and artefacts. Everything
else (the screenshots and the GIF) is a real capture of `examples/demo-vault` in Obsidian.

## `hero.jpg`

Model: `gpt-image-2`

> A wide, calm editorial illustration for an open-source project about a personal knowledge vault. Deep indigo-to-midnight background. In the centre, a softly glowing network graph of round nodes joined by thin light lines: a few large gold hub nodes, green and blue nodes around them, a cluster of small grey nodes, and a couple of hollow ring nodes at the edges. Beneath the graph, three translucent glass folders in a row, gently lit from inside (one warm green, one blue, one muted red), each sending a thin line of light up into the graph. Subtle starfield depth, soft bloom, clean vector-like shapes, lots of negative space on the left and right. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, words, captions, watermarks, logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
