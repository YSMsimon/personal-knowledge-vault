# Autopay Beats Remembering

Every fixed bill is on autopay a few days before it is due, so a busy week never means a late fee. It pairs with [[Emergency Fund Covers Three Months]]: autopay only works if the account never runs dry. Rent is the one exception for now, see [[Rent Is Due On The First]].
